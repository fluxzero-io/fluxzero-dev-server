# Helmet controls

The selected design is the front peak with ear defenders. Open
[fluxzero-helmet-configurable.blend](../fluxzero-helmet-configurable.blend)
for its editable Collections. The file contains a Dutch `LEESMIJ — Variaties`
text block with the controls.

- **Oorkappen**: enable or disable the ear defenders; enabled by default.
- **Korte klep**: the selected front peak and matching ribs; keep enabled.
- **Logo**, **Helm**, **Camera en licht**: shared components; keep enabled.

The Collection checkbox affects both viewport and render. The eye and camera
icons affect those separately. No add-on or script execution is needed to
switch the ear defenders. Use F12 to render the model.

The larger oval ear cups sit lower and farther back on their suspension arms.
The right soft cushion rests slightly over the logo's front edge. The rigid
housings remain clear of the original mark. The whole helmet and accessories
share the raised position and forward tilt, exposing the top of the peak.

The current model, render and SVG are in the [parent directory](../README.md).
Follow its rebuild instructions. The old `earmuffs/` and `short-peak/` files
are earlier design explorations and are no longer regenerated. No new versions
without the front peak are produced.
